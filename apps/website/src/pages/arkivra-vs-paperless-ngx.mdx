## Why Arkivra Exists

Paperless-NGX is a mature, widely used document management project with a strong community. Arkivra is inspired by that ecosystem, but explores a different shape for open-source document work:

- **Vault-first organization**: Separate collections by workspace, team, project, or sensitivity.
- **Semantic retrieval**: Combine full-text search with pgvector-backed semantic search.
- **Document chat**: Ask questions against a document, a vault, or the full library.
- **Local-first AI workflows**: Use local models when you want AI features under your own control, with cloud models as an operator choice.
- **Modern TypeScript stack**: React, Hono, Drizzle, PostgreSQL, Docling, and background workers.

Arkivra is newer and still under active development. Paperless-NGX remains the better choice if you need a very mature production system today.

## Which Should You Choose?

Choose Paperless-NGX if you want a proven, battle-tested document management system with years of community knowledge, integrations, and operational examples.

Choose Arkivra if you want to experiment with vault-based collaboration, semantic search, local-first AI chat, and a modern TypeScript architecture that is still evolving.

Both projects are open source, and the document management space is better because people can choose the workflow that fits them best.

- **Paperless-NGX**: [paperless-ngx.com](https://paperless-ngx.com/)
- **Arkivra**: [github.com/Jasnan/Arkivra](https://github.com/Jasnan/Arkivra)
